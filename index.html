About the Project

Traditional music recommendation systems often struggle to distinguish genres that sound similar but have different meanings. In this project, I built a Multi-Modal Variational Autoencoder (Beta-VAE) that fuses Mel-Spectrograms (Audio) and DistilBERT Embeddings (Lyrics).

By enforcing disentanglement in the latent space, the model learned to separate genres with 67% better accuracy than standard deep learning baselines.
